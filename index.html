How I got here

Engineering taught me to test ideas, product design taught me to make them, and client work taught me to sell them.

  1. 2017–2021

    B.E. Automation & Robotics Engineering

    KLE Technological University, India

    Robotics, mechanical design and simulation in SolidWorks and MATLAB: a delta robot, an idol flaw-detection system and a battery management system for an electric vehicle. On the side I ran my own art brand and designed logos, packaging and album covers for paying clients, and was runner-up in Hero MotoCorp’s 2020 design challenge.

    See the engineering projects

  2. Mar 2021 – Apr 2022

    Product Design Engineer

    Intech Additive Solutions, Bengaluru. Joined as a design intern, promoted after six months.

    Designed products for metal 3D printing, from sketch and CAD in Rhino 3D and Grasshopper through to printing on SLM 280 machines and testing.

    • Faucets for HSIL: manufacturing cost down 20%, part volume down 25%.
    • Apple Watch strap prototypes: time to market down 40%, purchase-order turnaround down 35%.
    • Gyroid heat sinks: 2.3× the structural thermal performance, component failures down 18%.
    See the 3D printing work

  3. Jul – Dec 2022

    Client Servicing & Brand Activation Manager

    Shubharambh Productions, Bangalore

    The client’s main contact between the brand, vendors and our team: requirements, proposals, budgets, contracts and timelines, through to delivery on site, often on several events at once.

    • Exhibition design and brand activation for FILA’s seasonal launch at a major retail expo.
    • Exhibition set-up and sales display for Ralph Lauren’s new collection.
    • A nine-month employee engagement programme for Mondelēz.

  4. 2023–2024

    MA Design, Innovation & Brand Management

    University for the Creative Arts, London

    Brand strategy, trend forecasting, design thinking and decision analysis. My dissertation built an ethical AI framework for retail brands from theory, company cases and expert interviews.

    See the MA projects

    Sales Associate

    H&M, London

    The role grew well past the contract, into visual merchandising, business reviews, delivery coordination and coaching the team.
