My approach

From problem to possibility.

A technical foundation with a human and strategic perspective. The same five steps run through a faucet, a business pitch and a dissertation.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Research the problem, the people affected and the context.

    e.g. Personas; expert interviews in three countries

  2. 02

    Explore

    Look for patterns, alternative perspectives and opportunities.

    e.g. YouTube brand mind map; PESTEL

  3. 03

    Define

    Translate research into a focused problem or strategic direction.

    e.g. H2H value / effort matrix; How Might We

  4. 04

    Create

    Develop concepts, products, strategies or experiences.

    e.g. Faucet sketching, CAD and flow simulation

  5. 05

    Communicate

    Make the outcome clear, engaging and useful.

    e.g. H2H app mock-up and live pitch

Research establishes direction. Making tests possibilities. Communication makes the work accessible.
